Grouped event integration
Wizard creation stages a WorkEvent and its initial client booking in one
transaction, with sessions and eligible payment. A season creates separate
grouped events. Event championships aggregate booking series. Client booking
editing offers explicit add/reuse of multiple championships. Main's grouped
billing, weather/track-place data and session batch UI are retained.

Implemented behavior
- One event or season, required dates and optional multi-day range, suggested
  title or custom override, and selected optional sections with retained data.
- Client/project context with selected championships and explicit add/reuse;
  shared series with preserved per-event overrides, including none.
- Multiple collapsible sessions; copies append independent drafts. Outline
  copies omit times; timed copies shift civil dates while preserving local
  clock times, reject nonexistent DST times and reset schedule/coverage.
- Named timezones with date-aware offsets, separate work/session zones,
  MKLocalSearchCompleter suggestions and manual location entry.
- Exact native fee arithmetic, independent optional breakdown with explicit
  adoption, unknown versus zero, per-event season fees and individual receipts.
- Full event calendar at review, concise configured details and edit controls.
  Events, sessions and eligible single-event payment save atomically; failures
  roll back the transaction and retain the editable draft.

Series semantics
V9 extends booking series while preserving legacy singular membership.
Sessions still have a singular override. An unoverridden session inherits only
when its booking has exactly one series; multiple booking series do not silently
choose one or assign all championships to every session.
