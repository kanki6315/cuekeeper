Roles: two single-page directions

Settings → Roles today is a plain A–Z list. To see a role's events you open a second page, which also holds Edit. Both directions below put the events on the Roles page itself and keep renaming, adding and deleting there too. Shown on iPhone, light appearance, fictional data, today is Sat 17 Oct 2026. Both use the shipped Daybook pieces: date rails, booking badges, ruled panels and capsule glass actions.

A · Inline roster Roles stay the list. Each one opens in place.

This is the smallest change from today. Each role row gets a count and its next date, and a tap opens its events beneath it instead of pushing a page. The open role shows Coming up plus the two most recent past events. Show all 14 extends the same group in place, a year at a time. Several roles can be open at once. Rename works in the row, and swipe offers Rename and Delete, so the old detail page and its Edit sheet are no longer needed.

Good at

  • "What have I done as X?" One tap, no navigation.
  • Close to today's code: LibraryView keeps its list, and LibraryDetailView's query moves into the row.
  • Works for projects and series later with no changes.

Watch for

  • A long open role pushes the others off screen. That's why it shows four events plus Show all.
  • Comparing two roles over time means opening both.

B · Role lens One agenda of your work, filtered by role.

This flips the page to lead with events. A row of role chips sits above a single date-led agenda. All shows every event that has a role, tagged with the roles you held. Picking a chip narrows the agenda to that role and shows a summary: how often you did it this year as a 12-month strip, the clients involved, and what's next. Managing a role happens in the summary's ⋯ menu, which also offers Merge for duplicates like "Pit reporter" and "Pit lane reporter".

Good at

  • Comparing roles: switch chips and the agenda stays in place.
  • Shows what the role looks like over the year, not only a list of events.
  • Handles events with several roles honestly, using tags.

Watch for

  • Past ~6 roles the chips need horizontal scrolling. Search would also match role names.
  • The All view repeats a lot of the Events tab. It could default to the most-used role.
  • More new code: a summary model like ClientSummary.
