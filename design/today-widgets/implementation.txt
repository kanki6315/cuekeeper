Today and widgets — implementation handoff, October 15, 2026

Authority and scope
The approved concept-board-v2.png establishes the chronological day schedule,
compact next-call strip and event/client context. small-widget-list-concept.png
supersedes the earlier small-widget next-call-only proposal with a short list.
These are composition references, not pixel-exact native chrome requirements.
This extends the established Daybook / Combined Glass system: semantic SF type,
warm reading surfaces, forest/mint accents and native navigation. No visual
system change or new global token is required. DESIGN.md and
.impeccable/design.json are preserved, including unrelated historical drift.

Implemented surfaces
Upcoming offers Today, Week and All. Today shows the full chronological day,
including elapsed sessions, with start/end, call, event, client, confirmation
and coverage information. A compact next-call strip requires booked, confirmed,
covering work and uses the start when no call is saved. Session rows open their
existing detail; Today adds no directions or event-notes actions. Accessibility
text stacks the time and content columns. Empty days point to Week/All; events
without a day's sessions remain under Schedule to come.

Small widgets show up to two remaining sessions, sharing the event heading when
possible and labelling each event otherwise. Medium shows up to four; overflow
is counted. Inline, circular and rectangular accessories summarize the next
commitment or remaining work. All widget taps open cuekeeper://today, resetting
Upcoming's scope and navigation even when the app is already on a session.
There is no session-specific widget route or live-broadcast-status claim.

Time and model behavior
Today and widgets deliberately use the device-local day and displayed time,
labelled with the current timezone; a differing event timezone appears as a
secondary start-time readout in Today. Event/session stored instants are unchanged.
Calendar day boundaries account for DST; timed overnight sessions and sessions
whose call falls today remain included. Untimed sessions use their event-zone
day, falling back to the event date range when the session day is absent.
Cancelled/deleted work is excluded. Today's full list retains completed and
not-covering context; widgets exclude completed events, ended sessions and
not-covering sessions. Unknown end times remain eligible rather than inventing
an end. Tentative/undecided rows cannot supply the next confirmed commitment.

Shared snapshot
SchedulePublisher captures local event/session labels, dates, status and coverage
into an atomic schedule.json in group.com.arjunakankipati.cuekeeper. Fees, notes,
broadcast URLs and CloudKit credentials are absent. Related-record changes and
app activation refresh the snapshot; unchanged captures avoid unnecessary reloads.
Write failures retain the last complete snapshot. WidgetKit receives local call,
start, end and midnight entries plus a refresh request; the OS controls actual
reload timing. Widgets do not fetch iCloud independently or promise live sync.
Missing snapshots ask the user to open Cuekeeper. Content is marked privacySensitive;
actual Lock Screen redaction and tint behavior still require host verification.
