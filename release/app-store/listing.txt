Description
Your next event starts here.

Cuekeeper is a daybook for broadcast freelancers: commentators, presenters, producers and crew. Capture dates now, then add the details as your work takes shape.

KEEP YOUR WORK TOGETHER
Track tentative, booked, completed and cancelled events. Save clients, roles, locations and notes, and build a session schedule with call times and broadcast links.

SEE WHAT'S NEXT
Use Today, Week and All to find upcoming work. Home and Lock Screen widgets show a snapshot of your schedule. Work across timezones with event times and an additional local-time display for remote or hybrid work.

TRACK FEES AND PAYMENTS
Record agreed fees, track invoices and partial payments, and keep balances separate by currency. Cuekeeper tracks your records; it does not generate or send invoice documents, connect to banking, calculate tax or convert currencies.

KEEP RECEIPTS WITH THE EVENT
Scan or import a receipt, review its details, and save it as an expense. Automatic field extraction uses Apple's on-device model on compatible devices; manual entry remains available. Set up an optional Shortcuts Wallet automation to capture expense drafts for later review.

CONNECT YOUR CALENDAR
Publish event placeholders and eligible confirmed sessions to a calendar you choose. One designated device publishes changes when Cuekeeper is open and connected.

YOUR DAYBOOK, ACROSS DEVICES
Save work offline and sync through your private iCloud database when available. Create separate manual iCloud backups and recover recently deleted events. Sync and backups serve different purposes.

Requires iOS/iPadOS 27. Automatic receipt extraction requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available. iCloud features require an available Apple Account and connectivity. Wallet automation is configured by you in Shortcuts; available inputs vary. Widgets update on the system's schedule.
