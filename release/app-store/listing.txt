Description
Your broadcast work, from first booking to final payment.

Cuekeeper is a daybook for freelance commentators, presenters, producers and crew. Keep events, clients, call times and fees together on iPhone and iPad.

• Plan sessions and follow your schedule across timezones.
• Track agreed fees, invoices, partial payments and outstanding balances.
• Scan receipts and keep expenses attached to each event.
• Connect your calendar and see upcoming work in widgets.
• Work offline, sync with iCloud, and back up your records and receipts to iCloud or Files.

Start with the details you know and fill in the rest as plans take shape. No separate Cuekeeper account, advertising or tracking.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 27. Automatic receipt-field extraction requires compatible hardware with Apple Intelligence available. Invoice tracking does not include invoice document creation or sending. Exported backup files are not encrypted.
