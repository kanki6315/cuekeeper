Cuekeeper Support

Need help? Email cuekeeper@arjunakankipati.com.
Include your Cuekeeper version and build from Settings → About, your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Redact personal details from screenshots. Never send your Apple Account password, verification codes, or an entire daybook backup.

Getting started
Add an event with its dates, then fill in the client, sessions and fee as details arrive. The event timezone follows its location; set Home location in Settings for events without one. Upcoming's Now mode shows your next call and the next 72 hours; Plan lists gaps to settle and what is coming up. Booking status, session schedule confirmation, coverage, invoicing and payment are separate decisions. You can use Cuekeeper without connecting Calendar. Core daybook entry works offline; iCloud and online services require connectivity.

My records haven't appeared on another device
Check that both devices use the intended Apple Account and that iCloud is available. Open Cuekeeper on both devices and check the Your data panel in Settings for errors. A completed transfer on one device does not guarantee the other device has received it. Do not delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot unsynced work. Contact support if an error persists.

Calendar isn't updating
Only one device publishes Cuekeeper's entries. Open Cuekeeper on that device with connectivity and Calendar access so it can receive changes and reconcile them. Only eligible confirmed, covering sessions are published, alongside event placeholders. To transfer publishing, disconnect Calendar on the previous device before connecting on the new one. Do not delete unrelated calendar entries to troubleshoot. New entries are published without alerts.

Backups and recovery
Backups are separate, manual copies of your daybook. Save them in your private iCloud storage or export a ZIP file to Files. Each copy includes events, sessions, clients, financial history, expenses and receipt photos already on this device. Calendar connections and device preferences are not included.
Receipt images are stored as separate files inside the ZIP alongside your record details. Keep the ZIP intact when moving it between devices. You do not need to unzip it to restore.
Open Settings → Recovery & backups → Backups.
For iCloud, choose Back Up Now and wait for the saved confirmation. For a file copy, choose Export to Files, then choose a destination in the system file picker. If sync is still delivering changes, those changes may not yet be included.
To recover an iCloud copy, choose Restore beside it. To recover a file, choose Import a Backup and select the Cuekeeper ZIP. Import from Files is also available during first-launch recovery. Download a file in Files first if its storage provider cannot open it.
Wait while Cuekeeper checks the records and receipt files, then review and confirm Restore Backup. Restore adds missing records and recovers deleted records without replacing newer edits or payments.
For a recently deleted event or session, use Recently Deleted. Restore the parent event before an independently deleted session when needed.
Keep Cuekeeper open until the operation finishes. Preparation and checking show progress; iCloud transfers show a waiting indicator. Cancel is available during preparation and reading, but not while a restore is being saved or an iCloud backup is being committed.
Each archive is limited to 512 MB, including up to 32 MB of record details and 200,000 records. Keep up to 50 iCloud copies. You also need free device storage for temporary copies, unpacked images and restored records. If you reach a limit, contact support; do not delete financial history just to make space.
If a backup is missing files, damaged or unsupported, Cuekeeper rejects it before restoring. Keep another known-good copy and try again; do not edit files inside the ZIP.
Recently Deleted normally offers recovery for 30 days. Restored records sync to your other devices and can return to a connected calendar. Deleting an iCloud backup does not delete your current daybook or exported files. Remove exported copies separately from wherever you saved them.
Exported ZIP files are not encrypted. They contain personal and financial records and receipt images. Save them somewhere you trust and do not send a whole backup to support. Ending recovery is not secure erasure; see the privacy policy for retention details.

Receipt scanning
Choose Scan Receipt in Expenses, the Upcoming header or an event. Review the proposed fields before saving. If camera access is off, choose Open Settings to allow it, or use photo import. Automatic extraction requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready. If a receipt is too long for the on-device model, its recognized text (not the photo) is sent to Apple Private Cloud Compute, which needs an internet connection; Cuekeeper tells you when this happens. You can enter fields manually when extraction is unavailable or inaccurate. Keep Draft preserves incomplete work. A draft receipt holds up its event's invoice until finished. Business costs have no event, need no photo and are never rebilled; Repeats monthly offers next month's cost under To finish when due.

Wallet automation and reminders
Settings → Integrations → Wallet Automation explains setup in Shortcuts. Cuekeeper cannot create the personal automation for you. Available transaction fields depend on the system. Find captured drafts under To finish in Expenses, or from the drafts prompt in Payments. Separate purchases with identical amounts are not automatically merged. Reminders are optional; drafts remain accessible if notifications are denied or silenced by Focus.

Widgets
Add a Cuekeeper widget using your device's widget picker. Open Cuekeeper to refresh its schedule snapshot. Widgets show today's remaining sessions, then later days. The system controls widget refresh timing, so widgets may lag behind the app or another device. Check Home/Lock Screen privacy settings if you do not want schedule labels visible there.

Invoices and currencies
Payments shows each month's events against four steps: Fee, Client, Work and Receipts. An event is ready to invoice when its fee is agreed, its client has invoicing details, the work is done and no receipt is still a draft. Choose Invoice to pick what to bill, including finished reimbursable receipts, then record how it went out. Switch to Invoices for every invoice and payment across months. Cuekeeper tracks invoices, balances and payments. It does not generate or send invoice documents, access banking, calculate tax, or convert currencies. Balances are kept separate by currency. A partial payment against a grouped invoice reduces its invoice balance rather than assigning payment to a particular event.

Privacy and deleting data
Read https://cuekeeper.arjunakankipati.com/privacy for storage, processing and retention details. Removing an event from Recently Deleted ends recovery but is not secure erasure. Backups remain separate. Contact support for guidance before attempting to remove all app data; the developer cannot access your private iCloud daybook.
