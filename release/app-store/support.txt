Cuekeeper Support

Need help? Email cuekeeper@arjunakankipati.com.
Include your Cuekeeper version and build from Settings → About, your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Redact personal details from screenshots. Never send your Apple Account password, verification codes, or an entire daybook backup.

Getting started
Add an event with its dates, then fill in the client, sessions and fee as details arrive. Booking status, session schedule confirmation, coverage, invoicing and payment are separate decisions. You can use Cuekeeper without connecting Calendar. Core daybook entry works offline; iCloud and online services require connectivity.

My records haven't appeared on another device
Check that both devices use the intended Apple Account and that iCloud is available. Open Cuekeeper on both devices and check Settings → Storage & iCloud for errors. A completed transfer on one device does not guarantee the other device has received it. Do not delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot unsynced work. Contact support if an error persists.

Calendar isn't updating
Only one device publishes Cuekeeper's entries. Open Cuekeeper on that device with connectivity and Calendar access so it can receive changes and reconcile them. Only eligible confirmed, covering sessions are published, alongside event placeholders. To transfer publishing, disconnect Calendar on the previous device before connecting on the new one. Do not delete unrelated calendar entries to troubleshoot.

Backups and recovery
Settings → Recovery & backups lets you create a separate iCloud backup or recover an event from Recently Deleted. Backups are manual and include only data already on the device. Restore adds missing records and recovers deleted records; it does not undo newer edits or payments. Receipt photos and expenses are included. There is a 100 MB limit per backup and a limit of 50 saved backups. Deleting an old backup does not shrink the daybook included in a new backup. Contact support if you reach the size limit; do not delete financial records simply to make space.

Receipt scanning
Open an event's Expenses and choose Scan Receipt. Review the proposed fields before saving. If camera access is unavailable, use photo import. Automatic extraction requires a compatible device with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready. You can enter fields manually when extraction is unavailable or inaccurate. Keep Draft preserves incomplete work.

Wallet automation and reminders
Settings → Integrations → Wallet Automation explains setup in Shortcuts. Cuekeeper cannot create the personal automation for you. Available transaction fields depend on the system. Review captured drafts from Upcoming or Payments → Expenses to complete. Separate purchases with identical amounts are not automatically merged. Reminders are optional; drafts remain accessible if notifications are denied or silenced by Focus.

Widgets
Add a Cuekeeper widget using your device's widget picker. Open Cuekeeper to refresh its schedule snapshot. The system controls widget refresh timing, so widgets may lag behind the app or another device. Check Home/Lock Screen privacy settings if you do not want schedule labels visible there.

Invoices and currencies
Cuekeeper tracks invoices, balances and payments. It does not generate or send invoice documents, access banking, calculate tax, or convert currencies. Balances are kept separate by currency. A partial payment against a grouped invoice reduces its invoice balance rather than assigning payment to a particular event.

Privacy and deleting data
Read https://cuekeeper.arjunakankipati.com/privacy for storage, processing and retention details. Removing an event from Recently Deleted ends recovery but is not secure erasure. Backups remain separate. Contact support for guidance before attempting to remove all app data; the developer cannot access your private iCloud daybook.
