Cuekeeper Support

Need help? Email cuekeeper@arjunakankipati.com with your Cuekeeper version and build (Settings → About), device model, iOS/iPadOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Redact personal details from screenshots. Never send your Apple Account password, verification codes or a whole daybook backup.

Getting started
Add an event with its dates, then add the client, sessions and fee as details arrive. The timezone follows the event's location, or your Home location in Settings. In Upcoming, Now shows your next call and the next 72 hours; Plan lists gaps to settle. Booking, session confirmation, coverage, invoicing and payment are tracked separately. Entry works offline and without Calendar; iCloud and online services need a connection.

My records haven't appeared on another device
Check that both devices use the intended Apple Account with iCloud available, are online and have Cuekeeper open, then check the Your data panel in Settings for errors. A finished transfer on one device doesn't mean the other has received it. Don't delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot unsynced work. Contact support if an error persists.

Calendar isn't updating
One device publishes event placeholders and confirmed, covering sessions. Open Cuekeeper on that device while online so it can pick up changes. New entries have no alerts. To move publishing, disconnect Calendar on the old device before connecting the new one. Don't delete unrelated calendar entries to troubleshoot.

Backups and recovery
In Settings → Recovery & backups → Backups, choose Back Up Now to save to your private iCloud storage, or Export to Files for a ZIP. Backups include events, sessions, clients, financial history, expenses and receipt photos on this device, but not Calendar connections or device preferences. To restore, choose Restore beside an iCloud copy or Import a Backup. Cuekeeper checks the backup first, then adds missing and deleted records without overwriting newer edits or payments. Keep the ZIP intact and the app open until it finishes. Each archive is limited to 512 MB and 200,000 records, and you can keep 50 iCloud copies. For a single deleted event or session, use Recently Deleted (normally 30 days). Exported ZIPs aren't encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust.

Receipts and expenses
Choose Scan Receipt in Expenses, the Upcoming header or an event, then review the suggested fields. If camera access is off, choose Open Settings or import a photo. Automatic extraction needs Apple Intelligence on a compatible device. If a receipt is too long for the on-device model, its recognized text (not the photo) is sent to Apple Private Cloud Compute, which needs a connection; Cuekeeper tells you when this happens. You can always enter fields manually. Keep Draft saves incomplete work, but a draft receipt holds up its event's invoice. Business costs have no event, need no photo and are never rebilled; Repeats monthly offers next month's cost under To finish.

Wallet automation and reminders
Settings → Integrations → Wallet Automation explains how to set up the Shortcuts automation; Cuekeeper can't create it for you. Captured drafts appear under To finish in Expenses and in Payments. Separate purchases with identical amounts aren't merged. Reminders are optional; drafts stay in the app if notifications are denied or silenced by Focus.

Widgets
Add a widget from your device's widget picker. Widgets show today's and upcoming sessions from the app's last snapshot, so open Cuekeeper to refresh them; the system controls refresh timing. Check Home/Lock Screen privacy settings if you don't want schedule labels visible there.

Invoices and payments
Payments shows each month's events against four steps: Fee, Client, Work and Receipts. An event is ready to invoice once its fee is agreed, its client has invoicing details, the work is done and no receipt is still a draft. Choose Invoice to pick what to bill, including finished reimbursable receipts. The Invoices view lists every invoice and payment. Balances stay separate by currency, and a partial payment reduces the invoice balance rather than a particular event's. Cuekeeper doesn't create or send invoice documents, access banking, calculate tax or convert currencies.

Privacy and deleting data
See https://cuekeeper.arjunakankipati.com/privacy for storage, processing and retention details. Ending recovery in Recently Deleted isn't secure erasure, and backups are kept separately. Contact support before removing all app data; the developer can't access your private iCloud daybook.
